Features

Everything you need to manage your money

Simple, powerful, and private. No subscriptions, no cloud — just you and your finances.

Multi-Account Tracking

Manage bank accounts, cash, and wallets in one place. See all your balances at a glance.

Monthly Summary

See your spending patterns with pie charts and category breakdowns. Know where your money goes.

Custom Categories

Organize transactions your way. Create color-coded categories for income and expenses.

100% Private

Your data never leaves your computer. No cloud, no servers, no tracking. It's your data.

Arabic & English

Full bilingual support with RTL layout. Switch between Arabic and English with one click.

EGP Currency

Built for Egyptian Pound with proper formatting. Track your finances in your local currency.
